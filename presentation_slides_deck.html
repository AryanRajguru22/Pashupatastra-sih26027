1. The Railway Problem & Darshini's Mission

Context & Objectives
THE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING
  • Shared Track Capacity: High-speed passenger express trains and heavy freight operate on the same tracks.
  • Maintenance Possessions: Track renewals and repairs require complete line closure (possession block).
  • Scarce Windows: Section controllers have very limited time slots (mostly nighttime and midday off-peak).
  • Daily Disruptions: Unplanned rail fractures, OHE faults, and train delays frequently throw static schedules into chaos.
DARSHINI'S MISSION (DOMAIN + DATA)
  • Formal Domain Review: Establish mathematical & operational railway rules in DOMAIN_REVIEW.md.
  • Protect Shared Contracts: Maintain zero-breaking-change data contracts for the whole team.
  • Synthetic Data Generator: Build seed-driven generator (generator.py) producing realistic corridors.
  • Provide Standard Fixtures: Export Scenarios A, B, and C as benchmark datasets for all teammates.

3. The 5 Core Domain Freeze Decisions

DOMAIN_REVIEW.md
Domain Area Agreed Phase 1 Standard Operational Reason & Team Impact
1. Track Modeling Retain string track_id ("UP-1", "DOWN-1"). Attach geographic metadata in data layer. Zero solver breaking changes. CP-SAT interval scheduling stays fast; UI gets rich section & direction grouping.
2. Maintenance Categories 6 Indian Railways work types: Track Renewal, Tamping, OHE, Signalling, Inspection, Emergency. Matches physical operations, machinery needs, and precedence constraints.
3. Machinery Constraints Shared heavy machines modeled via mutual_exclusion_group (e.g. BCM_01, CSM_01). Guarantees a machine is never scheduled in two places simultaneously, avoiding solver timeouts.
4. Train Timetables Modeled as Discrete Possession Windows (Night, Midday, Evening slots) over 24h horizon. Solver enforces all scheduled blocks lie strictly within valid possession slots.
5. Disruption Scenarios 4 deterministic disruption triggers: Track Blockage, Emergency Work, Window Curtailment, Machine Breakdown. Enables reproducible PLAN → DISRUPT → RECOVER demo flow on stage.

24-HOUR POSSESSION WINDOWS (1440 MIN)
  • 🌙 Night Traffic Block (00:30 – 05:00 | 0030–0300 min):
    Heavy track renewals (CTR), ballast cleaning, and tamping.
  • ☀️ Midday Maintenance Slot (11:30 – 14:30 | 0690–0870 min):
    USFD flaw inspection, OHE power blocks, and tower wagon runs.
  • 🌆 Evening Off-Peak Slot (21:00 – 23:30 | 1260–1410 min):
    Signalling point machines and track circuit overhauls.
  • ⏱️ Headway Buffer: 15 minutes safety separation between consecutive blocks on the same track.
